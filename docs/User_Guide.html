1. Overview & Introduction

BetterPresser is a high-precision, 64-bit transparent digital audio compressor designed for mixing, mastering, and live sound production. Combining an intuitive vintage hardware console aesthetic inspired by classic analog processors with mathematical digital transparency, BetterPresser delivers clean dynamic control without unwanted harmonic distortion or coloration.

Key Features

  • Transparent Dynamic Control: Clean feed-forward gain computer with continuous second-order polynomial soft-knee.
  • Dual Detection Engines: Instantaneous Peak detection for aggressive transient control vs. continuous RMS energy integration for smooth vocal and bus leveling.
  • Dual-Mode Visualizer Display:
    • Analog VU Meter: Illuminated vintage dial with physical 2nd-order mass-spring-damper needle ballistics.
    • Live Scrolling Graph: Smooth, eye-comfortable waveform visualizer displaying Input, Output, and Gain Reduction curves over a ~3.3-second history window.
  • Precision Sidechain High-Pass Filter: 2nd-order SVF (20 Hz – 500 Hz) to eliminate sub-bass pumping, with a dedicated Sidechain Listen monitor mode.
  • Parallel Compression: Sample-exact Dry/Wet crossfader for New York style parallel compression.
  • Precision Level Metering: Vertical stereo Input and Output level meters with peak hold bars and clip warning indicators.

5. Practical Mixing Recipes & Presets

1. Punchy Snare & Drum Bus

Detection: Peak • Threshold: −18 dB • Ratio: 4.0:1 • Attack: 25 ms • Release: 80 ms • Knee: 3 dB • SC HPF: 60 Hz • Mix: 100%
Result: Lets the initial drum punch hit hard while clamping down on the ringing tail for punch and presence.

2. Transparent Lead Vocal Leveling

Detection: RMS • Threshold: −24 dB • Ratio: 2.5:1 • Attack: 10 ms • Release: 150 ms • Auto Release: On • Knee: 12 dB • Auto Gain: On • SC HPF: 100 Hz
Result: Smooth, invisible dynamic riding that glues the vocal at the front of the mix without audible pumping.

3. Master Bus Glue

Detection: RMS • Threshold: −12 dB • Ratio: 1.5:1 to 2.0:1 • Attack: 30 ms • Release: 100 ms • Knee: 6 dB • SC HPF: 85 Hz • Mix: 100%
Target: Aim for 1.0 to 2.5 dB of Gain Reduction on the analog VU meter to knit the mix elements together.

4. Aggressive New York Parallel Compression

Detection: Peak • Threshold: −32 dB • Ratio: 12.0:1 • Attack: 0.5 ms • Release: 40 ms • Knee: 0.0 dB • Mix: 35%
Result: Heavily crushes drum transients in parallel, injecting massive room body underneath the dry punch.
